# App Store and Google Play copy

All fields below are written to the limits Apple and Google actually enforce, with
counts verified. Two rules from the ASO brief drive the structure: the three
indexed Apple fields carry zero duplicate words between them, and no field wastes
characters on words Apple indexes automatically (the app name, the developer name,
the category, or filler like "app", "free", "best").

Product facts these are built on, so nothing here overclaims:

- Live channels in this build are **Shopify, eBay, Square and Facebook** only
  (`EXPO_PUBLIC_ENABLED_PLATFORMS=shopify,ebay,square,facebook`). Copy never
  implies more. Competitor names appear only in the hidden keyword field, which
  is allowed and is not a claim of integration.
- Pricing comes from real sold comps, which the app shows on the listing screen
  ("~6 days, 12 comps"). That is a supportable claim.
- There are **no awards, no press quotes, no ratings and no user counts yet**, so
  nothing in here invents social proof. See the note on screenshot 3.

### The trust-signal slot

The ASO brief puts a trust signal at position 3 because it is the highest-value
slot after the first. **Anorha has nothing honest to put there yet**: no award, no
press, no ratings, no user count. Inventing one is the single fastest way to lose
a store listing, so the slot is deliberately empty.

Three honest ways to fill it, in order of how quickly you could have them:

1. **A capability people distrust until they see it.** "Priced from real sold
   comps" with the comps visible on screen. Not social proof, but it answers the
   objection that pricing is guesswork.
2. **A real number from your own data**, once you have one worth showing and can
   stand behind it. Items listed, hours saved, oversells prevented. It has to be
   measured, not estimated.
3. **A real review**, after launch. Then it becomes a genuine trust signal and
   this slot earns its place.

Until one of those exists, run seven screenshots. Seven honest ones beat eight
with a fabricated badge.

### Preview video

Worth doing, and mostly skipped by competitors. Fifteen to twenty seconds, no logo
intro, text overlays because it autoplays muted. The obvious cut is the one the
product already tells: photograph the item, watch the listing come back priced,
then show one number changing across four channels at once.

## Open questions for you

1. **"AI" is missing on purpose.** Resellers do search it, so it is real
   volume. But your standing rule is that copy never names the machinery and
   always describes the outcome, and every line above follows that. Adding `ai`
   to the keyword field would be the compromise: hidden from users, visible to
   the algorithm. Your call.
2. **Channel list.** Copy says Shopify, eBay, Square, Facebook because that is
   what the build enables. The landing page marquee shows more. If more are
   genuinely shipping before submission, tell me and I will widen the copy; if
   not, the marquee is the thing that should shrink.
3. **Localisation.** The brief is right that extra locales are close to free
   keyword surface. Worth doing, but only with real per-market research, not
   translated English. Say the word and I will draft Spanish (Mexico), French
   (Canada) and Portuguese (Brazil) as separate keyword sets rather than
   translations.
